# Recommendations

## 1. Move month-to-month customers to annual contracts
- **Evidence:** month-to-month churn is 42.71% against 11.27% (one year) and 2.83% (two year). These customers hold about $308.8K of the $690.8K at risk.
- **How:** offer a discount, free upgrade, or price lock for a 12-month commitment, and target the highest-risk month-to-month customers first.
- **Track:** share of customers on annual contracts, and month-to-month churn rate.

## 2. Fix the first 12 months
- **Evidence:** 47.44% of customers in their first year churn, against 9.51% after 49 months.
- **How:** welcome call in the first month, a service check at months 3 and 6, and a clear first-bill explanation.
- **Track:** churn rate for the 0-12 month group.

## 3. Improve support and fiber optic experience
- **Evidence:** the top reason for leaving is the attitude of support staff (192 customers). Fiber optic churn is 41.89% against 18.96% for DSL. No tech support is 41.64% against 15.17% with it.
- **How:** support training, a first-contact resolution target, a review of fiber optic installation and speed complaints, and a tech support bundle.
- **Track:** support-related churn reasons, and fiber optic churn rate.

## 4. Work the at-risk list by value
- **Evidence:** 950 active customers are flagged high-risk, worth $690,811 a year.
- **How:** the Priority list on the Action Plan page is sorted by lifetime value. Call the top customers first and log the outcome.
- **Track:** how many contacted customers stay.

## 5. Encourage automatic payment
- **Evidence:** electronic check customers churn at 45.29%, against 15% to 19% for the other three methods.
- **How:** a small bill credit for switching to card or bank autopay.
- **Track:** churn among customers who switch.

## Expected impact

| Churn reduction | Annual revenue recovered |
|---|---|
| 5% | $83,479 |
| 10% | $166,957 |
| 20% | $333,914 |

These figures come from the what-if slider and assume churn reduction recovers the same share of lost revenue. Run a pilot on one segment first and measure the real result before scaling.

## Next steps

2. Add monthly snapshots of the data so churn can be tracked over time.
3. Build a predictive model to replace the source churn score.
